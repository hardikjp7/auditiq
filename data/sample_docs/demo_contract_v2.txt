ENTERPRISE DATA PROCESSING & FINANCIAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
TechCorp Solutions Ltd. | Version 2.1 | June 2026

SECTION 1: DATA COLLECTION AND LAWFUL BASIS
The Company processes personal data under Article 6 of GDPR on the basis of
contractual necessity and legitimate interests. All data subjects are informed
of the legal basis at the point of collection via our privacy notice.

SECTION 2: DATA RETENTION
Customer personal data is retained indefinitely for internal analytics and
marketing purposes. No formal deletion schedule has been established.
Data is shared with third-party advertising partners without explicit consent.

SECTION 3: DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
In the event of a personal data breach, the Company will notify the relevant
supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware. Affected individuals
will also be notified without undue delay where the breach poses a high risk.
A breach response team and documented incident response plan are maintained.

SECTION 4: INTERNAL FINANCIAL CONTROLS
The Company does not maintain formally documented internal controls over
financial reporting. No independent auditor review has been conducted
for the current fiscal year. ICFR assessments are performed informally.

SECTION 5: FINANCIAL RECORD RETENTION
All financial records, audit workpapers, and transaction logs are retained
for a minimum period of 7 years in accordance with SOX requirements.
Records are stored in encrypted, access-controlled repositories.

SECTION 6: WHISTLEBLOWER POLICY
Employees are encouraged to report concerns to their direct manager only.
No formal anonymous whistleblower channel or retaliation protection
policy exists at this time.

SECTION 7: CUSTOMER IDENTITY VERIFICATION
Basic email verification is used for standard account creation. For
high-value transactions exceeding $10,000, enhanced due diligence
is performed. However, no formal AML screening process is documented.

SECTION 8: CAPITAL ADEQUACY
The Company maintains capital reserves as per regulatory minimums.
Quarterly solvency assessments are conducted by the finance team
and reviewed by the board. Capital adequacy ratios are reported annually.

SECTION 9: CLAIMS PROCESSING
Insurance claims are acknowledged within 10 business days of receipt.
Resolution targets are set at 45 business days, exceeding the 30-day
regulatory requirement. A claims tracking system is in place.

SECTION 10: THIRD PARTY DATA PROCESSORS
Customer data is shared with 8 external vendors. Data processing
agreements compliant with GDPR Article 28 have been established
with 5 of the 8 vendors. Remaining agreements are under review.

SECTION 11: PROTECTED HEALTH INFORMATION
The Company handles limited PHI for employee benefits administration.
Access is restricted to HR personnel only. However, no formal
BAA agreements exist with our benefits vendors, and audit logging
for PHI access has not been implemented.

SECTION 12: PAYMENT CARD DATA
Customer payment transactions are processed via a PCI-DSS certified
third-party payment gateway. The Company does not store raw PAN data.
Network segmentation between payment and internal systems is maintained
via firewall rules reviewed quarterly.

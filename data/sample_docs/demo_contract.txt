FINANCIAL SERVICES DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT
Company: FinServ Solutions Ltd.
Date: June 2026

1. DATA COLLECTION AND LAWFUL BASIS
The Company collects personal data including name, address, financial history,
and transaction records. Data is processed under legitimate interests for fraud
prevention and service delivery as per Article 6 of GDPR.

2. DATA RETENTION POLICY
Customer personal data is retained indefinitely for internal analytics and
marketing purposes. No deletion schedule has been established. Data is shared
with third-party advertising partners without explicit customer consent.

3. BREACH NOTIFICATION
In the event of a data breach, the Company will notify affected customers
within 30 days. No procedure exists for notifying the supervisory authority.

4. INTERNAL FINANCIAL CONTROLS
The Company does not maintain documented internal controls over financial
reporting. No independent auditor review has been conducted for the current
fiscal year.

5. RECORD RETENTION
Financial records are retained for 3 years then destroyed. Audit workpapers
are not separately retained.

6. CUSTOMER IDENTITY VERIFICATION
Basic email verification is used for account creation. No formal KYC or
AML screening process is in place for high-value transactions.

7. CAPITAL REQUIREMENTS
The Company maintains cash reserves at management discretion. No formal
capital adequacy assessment has been performed against regulatory minimums.

8. CLAIMS PROCESSING
Insurance claims are reviewed within 45 business days. No formal
acknowledgment process is defined for incoming claims.

9. WHISTLEBLOWER POLICY
Employees are encouraged to report concerns to their direct manager.
No formal whistleblower protection policy or anonymous reporting
channel exists.

10. THIRD PARTY DATA PROCESSORS
Customer data is shared with 12 external vendors. Data processing
agreements have not been established with all vendors.
